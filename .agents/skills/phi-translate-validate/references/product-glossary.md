# Phi product glossary (ratified renderings)

Regenerated from the shipped Phi i18n catalogs on 2026-10-15 (public catalog, 5 sections, 8 locales). These renderings are BINDING when content names a product feature: reuse them verbatim so marketing, help pages and UI read as one product. A term missing here is looked up in the live catalog of the locale, never invented.

## Keep in English everywhere

Phi Browser, Phi, Phi Cloud, Phi Link, Phi Sentinel, Phinomenon, Sentinel, phi-agent, Phi Agent, Anamnesis, Chatbar, Memory Nebula, Memory Galaxy, OpenAI, ChatGPT, Codex, Hugging Face, Ollama, OpenRouter, MLX, Telegram, Bitwarden, Chrome, Chromium, Arc, Safari, RAG, Bifrost, Kiosk, Peek, Video Gist (feature brands).

Also: "Time Machine" stays English in ja/ko/de/fr/es/nl but is 时间机器 (zh-Hans) and 時光機 (zh-Hant), matching Apple's own UIs. When in doubt about an Apple brand, check Apple's support pages in that locale.

## Open ratifications (renderings in use, awaiting the owner's ruling)

- zh-Hans "Skills": catalog ships 技能 in places while the glossary rules Skill stays English in zh (divergence open since 2026-08).
- ja "mute": the app ships ミュート; Apple's own term is 消音; new strings follow the app for consistency pending a ruling (2026-10-12).
- "Custom" button: ko 커스텀 vs 맞춤, de Anpassen vs Eigene (2026-10-12).
- "default profile" (2026-10-15): coined per locale from the shipped "default Space" pattern (默认个人资料 / 預設設定檔 / デフォルトのプロファイル / 기본 프로필 / Standardprofil / profil par défaut / Perfil predeterminado / standaardprofiel).
- "library" noun, "Take control" family, nl Active vs Gepland, sentinel zh-Hant register: see the owner review pile.

- zh-Hans "Mini Player": the catalog ships 迷你播放程序 while zh-Hant ships 迷你播放器; the component owner was asked which zh-Hans form they prefer (2026-10-12).
